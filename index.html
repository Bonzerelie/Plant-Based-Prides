Plant-Based Prides

A movement to safeguard the global LGBTQ+ community from the effects of climate change

The LGBTQ+ community is statistically at greater risk of harm from climate change. We encourage Pride organisation to prioritise plant-based catering at their events and meetings as one of the easiest and most effective ways to reduce their environmental impact, and so to safeguard our community.
