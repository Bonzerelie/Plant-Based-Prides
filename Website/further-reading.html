Sources and further reading

A selection of informative articles and peer-reviewed studies behind the claims made across this site — on the LGBTQIA+ community's exposure to climate change, and on the environmental case for plant-based food.

On the environmental impact of plant-based diets

These are the core studies behind the reduction figures used in our Impact Calculator.

  • Nature Communications (2024) — sustainability benefits of transitioning to plant-based alternatives Found greenhouse gas emissions reduced 30–52%, land use 20–45%, and freshwater use 14–27%, with a vegan diet showing the highest reduction potential. nature.com
  • Poore & Nemecek, Science (2018) — Reducing food's environmental impacts through producers and consumers The study behind Joseph Poore's assessment that a vegan diet is "probably the single biggest way to reduce your impact on planet Earth." science.org
  • Eisen & Brown, PLOS Climate (2022) — rapid global phase-out of animal agriculture Stanford / UC Berkeley modelling study: phasing out animal agriculture over 15 years could offset roughly 68% of CO2 emissions through 2100. journals.plos.org
