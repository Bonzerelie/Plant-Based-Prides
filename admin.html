Add a Latest News article

Fill in the form, click "Generate code", then copy the result into assets/js/news-data.js and upload it to GitHub. This page doesn't save or publish anything by itself — it just formats your article correctly.

Got images for this article? Upload each image file itself to the assets/images/news/ folder in your GitHub repository first (drag it in via the GitHub website works fine), then type its filename into the relevant field below — e.g. assets/images/news/my-photo.jpg. You can have one main image (shown at the top of the article and on the listing page) plus as many additional images as you like, dispersed through the article text.
