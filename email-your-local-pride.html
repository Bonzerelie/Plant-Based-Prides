Email your local Pride

Pride events are almost always run by volunteers, and contacting them can go a long way. Please always be respectful of the effort they make.

Finding your local Pride's contact details

Most Pride organisations list a general enquiries or "info@" email address on their official website, usually on a Contact or About page. If you can't find one there:

  • Check their social media bios (Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter) — many Prides list an email address directly, or accept direct messages.
  • Look for a "committee" or "board" page, which sometimes lists named organisers.
  • Search for your Pride in a national or regional Pride directory (for example UK Pride Organisers' Network members) if it doesn't have its own dedicated website.
  • If in doubt, address your email to "the organising committee" — it will usually reach the right people.

Three lines of argument

Individuals are empathetic to different arguments and it can be important to balance your personal convictions with what may be the most effective line of argument with whoever receives your email. Below are three template emails you are welcome to use in full or as a guide, though remember personal words will often land more effectively.

1. Environmental Responsibility

The LGBTQ+ community is statistically at greater risk from climate change — through higher rates of homelessness and greater difficulty accessing support in disasters, among other factors. Reducing reliance on animal agriculture, one of the biggest drivers of climate change, is one of the most effective ways to cut a Pride's environmental footprint.

2. Ethical Responsibility

Protecting marginalised communities is central to Pride's ethos. Animal agriculture commodifies living beings for profit, and by scale is one of the largest causes of suffering to a voiceless group. Fighting for the rights of those without representation is, in our view, a natural extension of what Pride already stands for.

3. Combined Approach

Each of these reasons resonates differently with different people. The combined template brings the environmental, climate-risk and ethical arguments together into a single, rounded case.

Template email

Pick a tab to switch between the three versions. Replace [x] with your local Pride's name, and add your own name at the end.

Copied! 
Subject: Considering plant-based catering at [x] Pride

Dear Pride Organiser,

I am writing with the hope of encouraging [x] Pride to help safeguard the global LGBTQ+ community, who are statistically at greater risk of harm due to climate change, by reducing [x] Pride's reliance on animal agriculture and prioritising plant-based food options where possible – an easy way to reduce [x] Pride's environmental impact.

Several recent studies have shown that the LGBTQ+ community is particularly vulnerable to climate emergencies for several reasons, including a much higher prevalence of homelessness, greater difficulty accessing appropriate (particularly gender-appropriate) care and resources in the event of disasters, and location and economic inequality among others. The science is also clear that animal agriculture is by far one of the biggest global drivers of climate change and environmental pollution, and reducing our reliance on it is statistically one of the easiest ways to substantially cut our environmental impact – something I feel we have a duty to do anyway, given the current state of the planet.

You can find more information and links to verified academic sources, along with a calculator showing roughly how much of a reduction in environmental impact [x] Pride could achieve, here: https://www.plantbasedprides.com/impact-calculator.html

Thank you so much for your time and for any consideration you can give this.

[Your name]
Copied! 
Subject: Plant-based catering and Pride's duty to the voiceless

Dear Pride Organiser,

I'm writing to ask [x] Pride to consider prioritising plant-based catering at future events as a way to reduce Pride's ethical impact on voiceless animals.

Central to the Pride movement is a drive to protect and empower marginalised communities – those without a fair say in decisions that affect them, and I'm truly grateful for the people who have paved the way to a world where the rights of the LGBTQ+ community have expanded so much in the last 50 years as a result of this. I also believe that this principle shouldn't stop at our own community and I feel we have a real opportunity to extend this mindset to others.

Animal agriculture commodifies living beings for profit, and the dairy industry in particular is built on the exploitation of animals' reproductive systems. By scale, it is one of the largest sources of suffering inflicted on any individual, voiceless group with trillions of animals killed each year.

I feel we have a real opportunity to extend the principles of Pride into the consumer decisions we make, and avoiding the exploitation and suffering of animals for our food at large events when other, often healthier and more environmentally friendly, options are available feels like a natural extension of what Pride already stands for, and I hope you will feel the same way.

You can find more information about the Plant-Based Prides campaign, along with an environmental impact calculator, here: https://www.plantbasedprides.com/impact-calculator.html

Thank you for taking the time to consider this.

[Your name]

Subject: Why [x] Pride might consider plant-based catering

Dear Pride Organiser,

I'm writing to encourage [x] Pride to consider prioritising plant-based catering at its events and meetings, for three reasons I hope will resonate.

Firstly, the LGBTQ+ community is statistically at greater risk from climate change, through factors such as higher rates of homelessness and greater difficulty accessing appropriate support in the event of disasters. Reducing our reliance on animal agriculture – one of the biggest global drivers of climate change – is one of the most effective ways to cut [x] Pride's environmental footprint and reducing our contribution to changes that disproportionately impact our community.

Secondly, given the current state of the planet, minimising the environmental impact of any large event feels like a responsibility we all share, regardless of who is most affected by it, and I believe any organisation that can do so should seek to reduce their impact on the planet. Reducing meat and dairy consumption is one of the easiest, fastest and most effective ways of doing so.

Lastly, Pride has always stood for protecting marginalised communities who lack a voice in decisions that affect them. Animal agriculture commodifies living beings for profit at enormous scale, and I believe standing against the suffering of the voiceless is a natural extension of what Pride already represents.

Together, these are why I hope [x] Pride will consider moving toward plant-based catering and away from meat and dairy. There is more information, along with a calculator showing the potential reduction in environmental impact and links to academic sources of information, here: https://www.plantbasedprides.com/impact-calculator.html

Thank you so much for your time and consideration.

[Your name]
